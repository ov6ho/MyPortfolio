challenge_01.sh

          
Decode the access key

          
We intercepted this token. Decode it and submit the access key.

          
TW9oc2VuX0N5YmVyU2VjXzIwMjY=

          

          
The trailing = is padding. Try atob("…") in your browser console.

          

        

        

          
challenge_02.sh

          
Caesar's transmission

          
Every letter in this message was shifted by the same amount. Recover the flag.

          
MSHN{zljby1af_pz_lclyf_dolyl}

          

          
The flag starts with FLAG. How far is F from M in the alphabet? Numbers and symbols don't move.

          

        

        

          
challenge_03.sh

          
Memory dump

          
This hex was pulled from a process's memory. Find the password and submit only its value.

          
70 61 73 73 3A 20 53 33 63 75 72 31 74 79 40 32 30 32 36

          

          
Each pair is one ASCII character: 70 is "p". In Python: bytes.fromhex("...").decode()

What am I?
